\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method{} shows that a meaningful subset of RAGEN-style multi-turn LLM agent RL can be reproduced on a single 8 GB consumer GPU, provided the system is aggressively engineered for memory and rollout throughput. The modular rewrite and optimization stack make 200-step 0.5B PPO and GRPO training feasible under constraints that are far below the original server-grade setup.

Scientifically, the six-run matrix gives a two-sided result. PPO directionally reproduces RAGEN's claims: vanilla StarPO is unstable, moderate variance filtering mitigates the failure, and overly aggressive filtering becomes stagnant. GRPO reveals the boundary of that mechanism. Without a critic, variance filtering no longer acts as critic-data selection; because GRPO advantages are group-relative z-scores, filtering mostly reduces sample count and update opportunities. In the observed setting, vanilla GRPO is therefore the strongest configuration.

The practical recommendation is accordingly narrow but useful: under 8 GB VRAM constraints for sparse multi-turn LLM agent RL, GRPO with no variance filtering should be considered before directly transferring PPO-oriented StarPO-S filtering. More broadly, echo-trap diagnosis should not rely on entropy alone. Reward, format compliance, and entropy must be interpreted jointly to distinguish unhealthy collapse from healthy mode convergence.
